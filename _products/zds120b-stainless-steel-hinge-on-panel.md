## Key Features:  

 

Key Features
🔥 90-Minute Fire Resistance  
Made from solid stainless steel, this hinge holds its structural integrity in high-heat conditions for up to 90 minutes. It helps keep fire-rated doors closed and aligned when it matters most.  
🛡️ Corrosion-Resistant, 72-Hour Salt Spray Tested  
The hinge has passed a 72-hour neutral salt spray test, showing reliable resistance to rust and oxidation. It performs well in humid, coastal, and high-moisture environments such as bathrooms, kitchens, and hospitals.  
🔁 Tested to 150,000 Cycles  
Rigorous cycle testing of 150,000 open-and-close operations confirms smooth, quiet, long-lasting performance with no loosening or play, even in high-traffic doorways.

The reinforced stainless steel structure carries heavy doors with ease and resists bending, sagging, and warping over time, so doors stay square and operate smoothly for years.

Fine-tune the door position after installation in three directions (height, width, and depth), so you get perfect alignment and even gaps without removing the door.

The wide opening angle gives full door clearance. It's ideal for tight spaces, easy access, and applications that need doors to open flat against the wall.
