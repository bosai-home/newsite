WHO WE ARE

Making Hardware Sourcing Easier

Bosai Homes is an independent trading and sourcing company specializing in door hardware and building hardware products.

We work with carefully selected manufacturing partners to help importers, distributors, wholesalers and contractors source practical hardware solutions for residential and commercial applications.

Our product range includes door handles, hinges, door locks, door closers, cabinet hardware and other related accessories.

We understand that growing businesses need more than just a product supplier. They need flexible ordering options, dependable product quality, competitive pricing and responsive communication. That's where we come in.
